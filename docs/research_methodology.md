# Research Methodology

## Research Approach

This project follows a Research-Based Learning (RBL) approach. The research starts with research questions and hypotheses, followed by data collection, analysis, and evaluation.

The project uses YouTube video data to investigate the relationship between upload time, negative user engagement, and video popularity.

## Research Question 1: Upload Time

The first research question investigates whether the upload time of a YouTube video is associated with its probability of becoming trending.

Videos will be grouped according to their upload time. The trending status of videos in different time groups will then be compared.

## Research Question 2: Negative User Engagement

The second research question investigates whether negative user engagement is associated with the virality of a YouTube video.

Negative user engagement will be analyzed together with video popularity indicators to examine whether there is a significant relationship.

## Hypothesis Evaluation

The project will evaluate the proposed hypotheses using statistical analysis and machine learning methods.

The analysis will be based on the collected YouTube data. The project will not assume the research outcome before analyzing the data.

## Expected Outcome

The project aims to determine whether upload time and negative user engagement are significantly associated with YouTube video popularity and trending status.
